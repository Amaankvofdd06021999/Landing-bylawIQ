## Scope

Marketing website with live, stored enquiry submissions. The separate interactive product demonstration uses prepared examples and fictional building bylaws, explicitly labeled in the interface. It does not connect to a generation model or ingest legal data.

Forms save to the private D1 enquiries table. No public read endpoint is exposed, and no outbound email or calendar booking is performed. Requests can be reviewed with Sites database tools by the site owner.
